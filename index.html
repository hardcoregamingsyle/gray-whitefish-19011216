About the Legend

Technoblade (born June 1, 1999 – June 30, 2022) was a Minecraft YouTuber and streamer known for his incredible PvP skills, dry humor, and his iconic pig persona. He rose to fame through his participation in the Minecraft Championship (MCC), SMP Earth, and Dream SMP, where he became a fan favorite for his tactical genius and memorable one-liners.

Beyond his gameplay, Technoblade was a beloved member of the Minecraft community. His family announced his passing in June 2022 after a battle with cancer, but his legacy lives on through his videos, quotes, and the countless lives he touched. The phrase “Technoblade never dies” is more than a meme — it’s a testament to his enduring spirit.
