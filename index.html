About Technoblade

Alexander "Technoblade" Quon was more than just a YouTuber - he was a legend who redefined what it meant to be a content creator. Known for his incredible Minecraft gameplay, sharp wit, and infectious enthusiasm, he captured hearts around the world.

From Bedwars dominance to Dream SMP storytelling, Technoblade left an indelible mark on gaming culture and inspired millions with his creativity and passion.

"If you're going through hell, keep going."

Legacy & Achievements

  • 2013 - Created YouTube channel, beginning of an iconic journey
  • 2014 - First viral video, gaining massive following
  • 2015 - Became known for exceptional Bedwars skills
  • 2018 - Joined Dream SMP, becoming one of its most beloved characters
  • 2019 - Reached 1 million subscribers
  • 2020 - Announced cancer diagnosis with remarkable grace
  • 2022 - Passed away peacefully on June 30th

Memorable Quotes

The Community That Remembers

Even after his passing, Technoblade's impact continues to grow. His community has rallied together, creating art, music, and stories that honor his memory. From charity streams to memorial videos, fans worldwide celebrate the life of the Crimson King.

His story reminds us all that true greatness isn't measured by time, but by the lives we touch and the inspiration we leave behind.
