Technoblade

The Blood God, The Blade, The Legend

1999 — 2022 · Forever in our hearts

In Memoriam

Technoblade (born Alexander) was a Minecraft legend, a beloved content creator, and a fierce competitor. He captivated millions with his skill, humor, and unyielding spirit. On June 30, 2022, he passed away after a battle with cancer, but his legacy lives on through his videos, his community, and the incredible impact he made on the world.

This tribute is a small token of gratitude from fans who will never forget the joy he brought us.

His Legacy

Technoblade's influence extends far beyond Minecraft. He raised over $500,000 for cancer research through charity streams, and his channel continues to inspire new generations of players. His determination, wit, and authenticity are a beacon for the gaming community.

🏆 PvP Champion

Multiple Minecraft Championship wins, Bedwars legend, and the undisputed king of Potato Wars.

❤️ Fundraiser

Raised over $500,000 for the Sarcoma Foundation of America and other cancer charities.

📺 Content Creator

Over 10 million subscribers on YouTube, with videos that combined humor, skill, and storytelling.

👑 Community Icon

His catchphrases, memes, and personality created a community that remains active and supportive.
